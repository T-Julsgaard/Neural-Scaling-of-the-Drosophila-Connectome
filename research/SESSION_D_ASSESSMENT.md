## Decision and strongest contribution

The strongest defensible contribution is a controlled boundary case: **in two specified, normalized connectome-derived feedforward models, bounded frozen participation calibration did not improve sequential associative retention beyond the tested ordinary tuning procedures; in the larval model, two diagnostic task families nevertheless retain useful information readable by a fitted linear readout.** This qualifies the use of participation statistics as a proxy for useful memory in these models. It does not discover that representations and learning procedures are different, or that sequential updates can interfere.

Potential novelty lies in this particular intervention/comparator/anatomy evidence package and its prospective adult follow-up, not in a new principle. Its value is modest but credible as a short computational/negative-result report with reusable evidence. A major neuroscience or general continual-learning paper is not justified. A positive prospective prediction alone does not establish originality; the prediction is consistent with familiar sequential interference. The narrower outcome in Session D's decision table applies even though C passes its numerical gate, because the explanation is familiar and the causal bridge remains incomplete.

No inspected mismatch invalidates the narrow primary numerical statements. The material problems are unsupported causal/comparator/generalization interpretations and incomplete submission packaging, not evidence that every reported result should be discarded.

The code also makes the familiar interference mechanism transparent: without clipping, the opponent difference updates as `w' = w + 2 eta x (y - x·w)`. For an old signed feature difference `d`, its margin changes by `2 eta (y - x·w) (d·x)`. Overlap, error sign and learning rate therefore jointly determine individual changes; participation alone cannot specify them. This is algebra of the existing learner, not a new theorem or proof of the empirical null's cause.

## Smallest repairs and submission decision

For the **narrow report**, no new campaign is essential. The immediate repair is a finite manuscript/reproducibility pass: generate the three planned figures from saved data, make a portable manifest of the exact raw inputs/archives needed for those figures, resolve the stale validator's schema, and have a researcher outside this automated workflow inspect the feedback/schedule accounting, extraction assumptions and claim boundaries. Preserve third-party licenses and distinguish local reproducibility from publicly available artifacts. This is a recommendation before submission, not an already completed independent validation or permission to upload.

If the intended claim is **failure of adequately optimized streaming learning**, the smallest specific scientific repair is a bounded development-only lower-rate sensitivity for the selected C blocked learner, including acquisition and final old/new accuracy. Since the lower-grid issue is known from confirmation, any newly selected setting requires fresh, separately frozen evaluation to support a new confirmatory claim. Do not retune the existing confirmation sample, test a collection of algorithms or silently replace the old result. This repair is unnecessary if the report retains “tested four-rate procedure.” It was not executed.

If the intended claim is **why compensation succeeds in A but fails in EXP-007/008**, neither more seeds nor the rate repair suffices. A controlled common-model bridge changing one nominated factor is missing. That is a different study; the smallest repair to the current paper is to remove that causal claim. No campaign is proposed merely to earn a stronger title.

Plain-language answer: we learned that modest activity balancing was not a reliable way to improve memory in these two artificial circuits, and that their tested learner can forget distinctions that the features still support. Forgetting, rehearsal and the importance of readout learning were already known. The quantified boundary case is worth preserving and possibly submitting as a modest empirical report; it is not a major discovery. Before submission, finish figures and reproducibility packaging and obtain outside scientific scrutiny. No more experiments are required for the restricted claims.
