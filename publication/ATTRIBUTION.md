# Attribution, acquisition and rights

This is the compact report-production package accompanying the public research repository. It includes project-written manuscripts, plots, plotting/build/audit scripts, derived result summaries and compact provenance records. It does not grant new rights over upstream data, code, fonts or papers. No blanket open-source or open-data license is assigned here on the author's behalf. Consult the original source terms before any public redistribution.

## Direct scientific dependencies

**Larval anatomy:** Eichler et al., Nature 2017, DOI 10.1038/nature23455. Acquire [Supplementary Table 1 ZIP](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fnature23455/MediaObjects/41586_2017_BFnature23455_MOESM3_ESM.zip), then extract `connectivity matrix table 1.csv`. The CSV SHA-256 must be `b9d06b052b0c4b91a7221b3dde9552b670bb81c034d0d3ed7fdb5e4a3b2cfc4b`. The included `evidence/research/reference_data_audit.json` records exact indices, orientation and exclusions. Use the publisher's source terms and check third-party exceptions before redistributing the original matrix. The main report uses its 40-PN/73-mature-KC left projection, not the complete nervous system.

Acquire that file at the pinned commit; do not substitute a newer annotation silently.

## Excluded project assets and acquisition

Large saved simulation NPZ files are distributed separately in the [academic report release](https://github.com/T-Julsgaard/Neural-Scaling-of-the-Drosophila-Connectome/releases/tag/academic-report-2026-09-21). Source freezes and the full code/record history are retained in the [research repository](https://github.com/T-Julsgaard/Neural-Scaling-of-the-Drosophila-Connectome). Follow its REPRODUCIBILITY.md to download and restore the original arrays using the committed SHA-256 manifest. Report-critical paths and hashes are also in `raw_array_checks.json` and the copied experiment artifact/stage manifests. Rerunning a campaign creates different artifacts and is not an acquisition substitute. Protocol and result copies under `evidence/experiments/` preserve the scientific design and prior outcomes. They do not form a standalone training runtime.

## Related work and production dependencies

Zou et al., Fly-CL, Litwin-Kumar et al., Robins, and Xie and Ocker provide related scientific context; the report asserts no code reuse from those papers. Figures 1-3 are newly drawn from this project's saved computational results. They do not reproduce publisher figure artwork.

ReportLab, pypdf, pypdfium2, Pillow, NumPy and Matplotlib are required software dependencies, distributed separately under their own licenses. The ZIP contains no installed environments. Cambria/Calibri are used from the Windows installation and are not redistributed as font files; the PDFs embed subsets for rendering. No personal cache, credential, API key or third-party bulk archive is intentionally included.
