## Finding and prediction

EXP-007 found calibrated participation and ordinary tuning equivalent within +/-3 percentage points on primary retention: -0.27 [-1.67, 1.13] pp. Increased participation alone did not deliver a useful memory gain. This is a bounded limitation, not established causal mediation.

**Primary transfer prediction:** in a separately reconstructed adult olfactory projection, the transferred bounded calibration procedure remains practically equivalent to equally budgeted ordinary tuning on final retention (entire simultaneous interval inside [-.03,.03]). The procedure may change participation without adding useful retained memories. A nonsignificant difference is insufficient. Also compare the uncalibrated high-activity reference and optimized shuffled calibration with the same interval family. The ordinary contrast is the designated transfer decision; the other two qualify interpretation, not independent anatomical replications.

An interval wholly above +.03 contradicts the predicted limitation by establishing useful benefit; wholly below -.03 contradicts equivalence through harm. Other intervals that do not fit inside the band are inconclusive unless they exclude the full equivalence band. Report directional effects separately. Even equivalent retention does not establish adaptation or tail equivalence.
