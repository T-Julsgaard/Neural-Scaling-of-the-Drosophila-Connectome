## Update rule

When a claim changes, preserve its ID, date, previous status and reason in the research log. Add new exact source locations, replication records and counterevidence. Use an experiment run ID only after that run exists. Suggested implications are interpretations; do not promote them to published evidence because they are written beside a citation.
