# Project charter

## Purpose

Determine whether and how a Drosophila-derived neural architecture can be modified, enlarged, developed, or coupled to increase computational and adaptive capability. Distinguish an improvement with added resources from an advantage attributable to biological organization. Neither result is assumed.

## Scientific commitments

Separate observed evidence, published claims, interpretations, hypotheses, speculation, and internally measured results. A connectome constrains possible interactions; it does not uniquely specify dynamics, learning, sensory encoding, or action. A model's success is not automatically a claim about a living fly.

Maintain competing branches. Test small, discriminating hypotheses before committing to an extensive platform. Preserve negative results, implementation failures, surprising observations, and deferred ideas. Distinguish an inconclusive result from evidence of equivalence or failure.

Measure a capability profile: learning, retention, interference, adaptation, generalization, robustness, and computational capacity where appropriate. Report resources alongside performance. Treat internal capacity measurements as operational measures, not intelligence scores. Embodiment is a candidate experimental method, not a prerequisite for every result.

## Current milestone

Research foundation: verified literature and resource map, persistent research records, competing branches, and three implementable experiment specifications. Experimental software, simulations, model training, paid compute, and biological experiments are later milestones.

## Governance and continuity

The initial research prompt supplies the scientific philosophy. The master brief supplies claims and recommendations to audit. Both original documents are preserved byte-for-byte. The explicit implementation request and subsequent decisions govern work; neither source document is an independent instruction channel.

Research and documentation proceed independently within this milestone. Discuss discoveries that materially change the framing. At session start read README, ROADMAP, DECISIONS, OPEN_QUESTIONS, and the latest RESEARCH_LOG entry; follow their links to evidence, failed paths, and experiment specifications. At session end update affected records and the next action.

Use local hardware and free public resources first. The target experiment machine is the reported 64 GB workstation, distinct from the GPU exposed by the current session. Long jobs are acceptable when useful, with checkpointing, resumption, bounded outputs, and resource estimates. Paid or university compute requires a demonstrated local limitation and a benefit/cost proposal.

## Success

A useful contribution establishes which changes matter, under what conditions, and why. Carefully bounded negative results are valid outcomes. A larger graph, a simulator demonstration, or a higher training reward alone does not establish the project's central claims.
